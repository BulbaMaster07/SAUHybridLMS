📖About
Dr. Deepa Sharma is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at South Asian University, New Delhi. With over 12 years of teaching and research experience, she specialises in machine learning, deep learning architectures, and distributed computing systems.

She received her Ph.D. from IIT Delhi in 2012 and has since published extensively in top-tier IEEE and ACM venues. She serves as a reviewer for the Journal of Network and Computer Applications and has guided 6 doctoral students to completion.
